# Balloon Arena Defense

**[Play now](https://egeozcan.github.io/arena-defense/play/) · [Visit the website](https://egeozcan.github.io/arena-defense/)**

Build a fleet of toy farm and construction vehicles, send them after waves of balloons, and spend
what you earn on bigger tools and better teamwork. Your vehicles drive and attack automatically,
but you can jump into any of them and take the wheel in first person.

Free to play in a desktop browser with WebGL, a mouse, and a keyboard. No account or installation
needed. Progress saves in your browser.

![A harvester popping balloons during fleet-wide Pop Rush](public/screenshots/pop-rush.png)

## Your first wave

1. **Buy a vehicle.** You start with $650. A harvester is a good first pick for the opening packs of
   low balloons. Choose it from the bottom tray or press **1**.
2. **Deploy it.** Click a clear spot in the arena to place the vehicle. Press **R** to rotate it or
   **Esc** to cancel placement. Only deployed vehicles join the wave.
3. **Check the briefing.** It shows incoming balloon types, their heights, and any gaps in your
   fleet's coverage. Select a deployed vehicle to move it or choose its targeting strategy.
4. **Start the wave.** Click **Start wave** or press **Enter**. Your fleet handles the driving and
   popping. **Space** pauses, and **1 / 2 / 3** select 1×, 2×, or 3× speed.
5. **Spend your earnings.** After the wave, the workshop opens with your results, upgrades, and the
   next briefing. Buy support vehicles, improve your fleet, then return to deployment.

Each wave gives you **three minutes of game time** to pop everything. Leftover balloons cost lives;
clearing a wave quickly earns a cash bonus. Survive the campaign to win, then continue in freeplay
if you want more.

## Meet your fleet

Every vehicle has a job. Combine low, mid-height, and high-reaching tools, and make sure something
can handle armor. These are the stock vehicles; upgrades can expand their capabilities.

| Vehicle       | Good at                                                      | Keep in mind                                                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Harvester** | Fast, wide cuts through low packs                            | Cannot hurt armor without Attack tier 3; needs help with higher balloons   |
| **Sprayer**   | Fast pursuit and cone-shaped spray through mid-height crowds | Weak terrain grip and reduced armor damage; cannot reach high-flyers       |
| **Excavator** | Heavy hits that deal full damage to armor                    | Slow movement and attacks; needs crowd and high-altitude support           |
| **Crane**     | Heavy hooks reaching balloons 3–12 m high                    | Cannot hit low balloons or hurt armor without Attack tier 3                |
| **Baler**     | Long-range bales that pierce lines of balloons               | Scenery blocks shots; weak against armor and cannot reach high-flyers      |
| **Blower**    | Herding airborne packs toward teammates                      | Low damage; cannot hit ground balloons or hurt armor without Attack tier 3 |
| **Bulldozer** | Broad blade sweeps through low, armored packs                | Short reach; must turn and close the distance                              |
| **Mixer**     | Ranged concrete splashes through mid-height groups           | Slow firing and reduced armor damage; needs high-altitude support          |

## Upgrades and abilities

Open the garage with **G** between waves. Each vehicle has four upgrade paths:

- **Attack:** harder hits, faster tools, and better armor damage.
- **Speed:** faster pursuit and tool operation, followed by overdrive upgrades.
- **Traction:** better handling on mud, rough ground, and oil, plus hay-pushing and traffic recovery.
- **Specialist:** equipment unique to that vehicle, such as a rear header, splash hooks, acid spray,
  or a gathering vortex. Tier 3 unlocks its special ability.

You can invest in **two paths per vehicle**: one can reach **tier 5**, while the other stops at
**tier 2**. The garage previews each upgrade and explains which choices it will lock. Vehicles can
be sold for **70% of their total purchase and upgrade investment**.

Buy global abilities in the garage, then use them during a wave from fleet view:

| Key   | Ability         | How to use it                                                |
| ----- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Q** | Gust of wind    | Drag across the arena to push balloons and lower high-flyers |
| **W** | Emergency boost | Click a vehicle to boost its movement and tool speed         |
| **E** | Pitchfork       | Click a spot in the arena to damage a cluster                |

Abilities use cooldowns; garage upgrades improve cooldowns and charges. Select a vehicle during a
wave to activate its unlocked Specialist ability or change its **Strategy**.

## Keep the pops coming

Chain **10 pops**, leaving no gap longer than **2.5 seconds of game time**, to trigger **Pop Rush**.
Your whole fleet moves **45% faster** and attacks **60% faster** for **five seconds**.

The gold meter tracks your charge and Rush duration, and the chain badge tracks your streak.
Pops during Rush keep the streak going without extending the boost. Round results show your best
chain and how many Rushes you triggered.

## Stay ahead of the wave

- **Read the next-wave warning.** Armor first arrives in round 6, high-flyers in round 8, and
  carriers in round 10. Save for the tools you need before those waves arrive.
- **Cover the cargo too.** Layered balloons split as you pop them. Carriers drop low balloons and
  hay, so a fleet built only for high targets will leave work unfinished.
- **Check height and armor together.** Reaching a balloon does not guarantee you can damage it.
  The briefing checks deployed vehicles and their upgrades, and suggests ways to fill gaps.
- **Leave room to move.** Vehicles need clear approaches to their targets. Hay, tight lanes, mud,
  oil, and rough ground can make a powerful tool slow to reach the action.
- **Adjust targeting during combat.** Choose Nearest, Strongest, Highest, Oldest, or Most lives to
  suit each vehicle's job. The live fleet panel shows what each vehicle is doing.

A pre-launch check warns about coverage gaps and undeployed vehicles. If all remaining balloons
are beyond your fleet's capabilities, **End wave** lets you accept the displayed life loss early.
You can still try to rescue the wave with abilities first.

## Arenas and difficulty

Choose the **Barn** or the larger **Yard**, then pick a difficulty from the **New run** settings.

| Mode       | Starting lives | Campaign length | What to expect                                                    |
| ---------- | -------------: | --------------: | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Easy**   |            150 |       20 rounds | Lower prices and forgiving waves; a good place to learn           |
| **Medium** |            100 |       40 rounds | Denser packs, faster drift, and tougher specialist balloons       |
| **Hard**   |             50 |       60 rounds | More pressure, earlier modifiers, and less room for coverage gaps |

All modes start with $650. Later waves introduce fleeing, regrowing, and additional armored
balloons. After victory, freeplay keeps the waves coming.

## Controls

### Preparation and fleet view

| Control                                  | Action                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1–8** during deployment                | Buy a vehicle from the bottom tray                                            |
| **Click a vehicle**                      | Select it to inspect, reposition during preparation, or change strategy       |
| **R** during preparation                 | Rotate the selected or placing vehicle                                        |
| **Esc**                                  | Cancel placement or an armed ability, close a panel, or pause an active wave  |
| **Delete / Backspace** during deployment | Return the selected vehicle to the garage without selling it                  |
| **G** between waves                      | Open or close the garage                                                      |
| **Enter**                                | Leave the garage for deployment, then start the wave                          |
| **Space** during a wave                  | Pause or resume                                                               |
| **1 / 2 / 3** during a wave              | Set simulation speed to 1× / 2× / 3×                                          |
| **V**                                    | Rotate the camera                                                             |
| **+ / −**                                | Zoom in or out                                                                |
| **Right-drag**                           | Pan the camera                                                                |
| **F** during a wave                      | Drive the selected vehicle, or the first deployed vehicle if none is selected |

### Take the wheel

During a wave, select a vehicle and choose **Jump in**, or press **F**. All eight vehicles support
first-person driving, and the rest of your fleet keeps fighting automatically.

| Control               | Action                                                              |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **WASD / arrow keys** | Accelerate, reverse, and steer                                      |
| **Mouse**             | Look and aim                                                        |
| **Hold left mouse**   | Operate the sprayer, blower, excavator, crane, baler, or mixer tool |
| **Space**             | Brake                                                               |
| **Shift**             | Focus your aim                                                      |
| **R**                 | Center the view                                                     |
| **C**                 | Switch front/rear view when a rear tool is installed                |
| **E**                 | Use the vehicle's unlocked Specialist ability                       |
| **Q**                 | Use a purchased Emergency boost on your vehicle                     |
| **Tab**               | Switch to the next deployed vehicle                                 |
| **F**                 | Return to fleet view and automatic control                          |
| **P / Esc**           | Pause and release the mouse                                         |

The **harvester's header and bulldozer's blade work automatically on contact**: drive into low
packs to pop them. With the harvester's Specialist tier 3 rear header, **C** switches which header
you face. **W** drives toward that side, **S** drives away, and steering follows your view.

Driver mode uses **1× speed** and restores your previous speed when you leave. Click the arena to
capture the mouse; if capture is unavailable, hold the right mouse button and drag to look. The
cockpit shows your tool limits, speed, personal pops, and an arena radar. Switching away from the
tab pauses driving.

## Saves and settings

Your run saves locally during preparation. Reloading during a wave returns to its saved
preparation state. Saves belong to that browser and site; clearing site data removes them.

Use the sound icon to toggle effects, the help icon for the in-game guide, and **New run** to
choose a different arena or difficulty. Camera effects respect reduced-motion preferences.
Round summaries can export JSON replays; there is no in-game replay viewer yet.

## Development

### Run locally

Requires **Node.js 22.12 or newer**; CI uses Node.js 24.

```sh
git clone https://github.com/egeozcan/arena-defense.git
cd arena-defense
npm ci
npm run dev
```

Open the URL printed by Vite, normally `http://127.0.0.1:5173/`. The landing page is at `/` and the
game is at `/play/`. The game runs entirely in the browser and needs no backend.

### Build and check changes

```sh
npm test          # Gameplay, determinism, and regression tests
npm run build     # TypeScript checks and production bundle
npm run preview   # Serve the production build locally
```

Additional tools for gameplay and performance work:

| Command                                        | Purpose                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `npm run balance`                              | Simulate Easy, Medium, and Hard campaigns in both arenas          |
| `npm run balance:frontier`                     | Check legal vehicle builds for dominance and ineffective upgrades |
| `npm run balance:hard-yard`                    | Run ground-path campaign comparisons across six seeds             |
| `npm run balance:hard-yard-air`                | Compare crane and blower upgrade commitments across six seeds     |
| `npm run balance:hay`                          | Analyze hay handling across vehicle builds                        |
| `npm run balance:terrain`                      | Analyze oil and rough-ground handling across vehicle builds       |
| `npm run perf -- --ticks=1200 --repetitions=3` | Measure simulation CPU time and deterministic checksums           |

For a focused campaign check, pass options to the balance runner:

```sh
npm run balance -- --mode=hard --arena=yard --seed=73429 --plan=expanded
```

Balance reports live in [artifacts/](artifacts/). They record fixed simulated strategies, and older
reports may reflect earlier game rules. See the [six-seed campaign audit](artifacts/hard-yard-seed-balance.json)
and [performance review](artifacts/performance-review.json) for recorded results. Measure rendering
performance separately in a browser.

### Where to make changes

- [src/sim/data.ts](src/sim/data.ts): vehicle stats, prices, upgrades, arenas, and wave generation.
- [src/sim/engine.ts](src/sim/engine.ts): fixed-tick physics, AI, combat, commands, payouts, and replays.
  Other modules in [src/sim/](src/sim/) handle driving, routing, traffic, terrain, and Pop Rush.
- [src/ui/](src/ui/): garage, wave briefing, fleet controls, driver HUD, saves, and styling.
- [src/render/](src/render/): arena scene, procedural vehicle models, animation, and effects.
- [tools/](tools/): regression tests, balance runners, benchmarks, and the vehicle icon studio.
- [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md): the original game design and broader intended scope.

The renderer reads simulation state; player actions enter a command queue. The simulation runs
at 60 Hz with seeded waves and deterministic Rapier physics. Replays record the starting run and
commands with their simulation ticks.

To regenerate vehicle icons, open `/tools/vehicle-icons.html` on the development server, bake the
stock models, and save the images in [public/vehicles/](public/vehicles/). The studio also previews
upgrade combinations.

This is a playable prototype. Collision shapes, ramps, and some tool effects are simplified;
later waves use seeded counts, and freeplay needs more playtesting. The physics package embeds
WASM, which makes the production bundle sizable.

### Deploy to GitHub Pages

[The Pages workflow](.github/workflows/pages.yml) tests and builds pushes to `main` and pull
requests. Successful pushes to `main` deploy `dist/`; pull requests validate the build. Manual
workflow runs can also deploy.

CI sets Vite's base path from the repository name. To preview the current repository's deployment:

```sh
npm run build -- --base=/arena-defense/
npm run preview -- --base=/arena-defense/
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:4173/arena-defense/` for the landing page or append `play/` for the game.
For a fork, enable **Settings → Pages → Source → GitHub Actions** and update account-specific
links in the README, landing page, and package metadata.
